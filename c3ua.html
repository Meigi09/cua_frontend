What is C³UA?

Curriculum Compliance & Concept Understanding Auditor

C³UA is GS Mont Kigali / APACE's internal platform for keeping every class on pace with the REB syllabus, spotting concepts students are struggling with early, and preparing candidates for NESA — all organised by Level, Class and Subject so nothing gets lost in a long topic list.

What you can do inside

Four roles, one shared source of truth: Admins, Teachers, Students and the Education Authority.

✅
Compliance Monitor

Teacher pacing vs. the MINEDUC calendar, filterable by Level → Class → Subject, with risk flags for anyone falling behind.

🧠
Concept Mastery

A heatmap of how well each class understands each concept, so interventions target exactly where they're needed.

📚
REB Curriculum Sync

Levels, classes, subjects and teacher guides scraped straight from elearning.reb.rw, always up to date.

📝
Lesson Plan Analysis

AI review of submitted lesson plans against the official CBC topics, with a compliance score and recommendations.

🎯
NESA Readiness

Live countdown and readiness scores for S3 and S6 candidates heading into the national exam.

💬
Student Feedback

Anonymous difficulty ratings from students, aggregated by topic to surface what's genuinely hard.
